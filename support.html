Common questions

Where are my projects kept?

On your iPhone, in the app's own folder. You can see them in the Files app under On My iPhone, LapseCraft, Timelapses. Nothing is stored online.

How do I back up or move a project?

In the Library, press and hold a project and choose Export Project. That makes one zip file with the video, the photos and your edits, which you can save to Files, AirDrop or send anywhere.

An Apply or an export stopped part-way

iPhone doesn't let apps render video in the background, so keep LapseCraft open on screen until it finishes. If it was stopped, open the same screen and tap Apply again: it carries on from where it stopped.

A shoot was interrupted

If the app was closed during a shoot, the frames it had taken are still there. Tap the project in the Library and choose Restore.

Saving to Photos was refused

Check that LapseCraft is allowed to add to Photos in Settings, Privacy & Security, Photos. Very large videos that Photos can't keep are flagged in the Export screen before you start; choose a smaller size, or use Share to save the file to Files instead.

I deleted a project by mistake

Deleted projects go to the Trash first. In the Library, tap the ⋯ button, then Trash, to restore one.

I'm running out of space

In the Library, press and hold a project and choose Storage. Reclaim Previews frees space without changing anything you see. Reclaim Rendered Cuts frees more: it keeps your original and all your settings, and the project's Rebuild button brings the edits back when you want them.
