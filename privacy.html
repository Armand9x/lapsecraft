LapseCraft Privacy Policy

Effective 13 October 2026

LapseCraft is a timelapse camera and editor for iPhone and iPad, made by Christopher Calvert. This page says what the app does with your information. The short version: it collects nothing.

What the app collects

Nothing. LapseCraft has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It never connects to the internet, so nothing you shoot or do in the app is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Your photos and videos

Everything you shoot, import and edit is stored on your iPhone or iPad, in the app's own folder. It stays there until you delete it or choose to send it somewhere yourself, for example by saving a video to Photos, using Share, or exporting a project.

Permissions

  • Camera. Used to take the frames of your timelapse. The app can't shoot without it.
  • Photos (add only). Used to save a finished video to your photo library when you ask it to.
  • Photos (read). Asked only if you choose Import Album. Used to show your albums and to copy the photos of the album you pick into the app. The app doesn't look at your library at any other time, and nothing it reads leaves your iPhone. If a photo in the album is kept in iCloud, iOS downloads it from your own iCloud Photos. You can say no and import a folder from the Files app instead.

The app doesn't ask for your location, microphone or contacts. Photos you shoot or import may carry details your camera recorded in them, such as the time and camera settings. Those stay inside the files on your device.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
